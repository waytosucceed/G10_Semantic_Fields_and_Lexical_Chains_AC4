Writing Activity:

Choose ONE of the topics below and write a paragraph of 80–100 words.

  • The morning of an important exam
  • The street on the morning after a big festival or celebration
  • An evening at home when the power suddenly went out

However, make sure that you:
Build at least one semantic field of three or more words in your text. AND never name the mood or emotion directly. For example, if your field shows nervousness, you cannot use "nervous", "scared" or "anxious".
